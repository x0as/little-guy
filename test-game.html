Click PLAY to start the game

Controls & Powers

Movement: WASD or Arrow Keys to fly around
SHIFT / Turbo: Hold to activate turbo boost & sonic boom
Click / SPACE: Fire laser beam from eyes
Power 1 - Laser: Beam with click, fires from eyes
Power 2 - Heal: Click in air to heal buildings
Power 3 - Backup: Helicopter drops allies
City HP: Total health of the city
Player HP: Your health (regenerates over time)
Wave Timer: Seconds left in current wave
Stars: Buildings destroyed
